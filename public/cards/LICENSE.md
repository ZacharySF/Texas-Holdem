# Vector Playing Cards

Byron Knoll released this artwork into the public domain. These are the 52 standard faces from SVG Playing Cards 1.3, with dark-palette paint and high-contrast rank indices; geometry is retained. Four-color variants change suit paint and use a deeper diamond-card surface for contrast. `art/` contains the four aces, 12 illustrated alternative court faces, and two jokers from the same public-domain archive, recolored for decorative home-panel use. Jokers never enter the Hold’em deck. The custom geometric back is part of contemprorary under the project MIT license.

- Author: https://byronknoll.blogspot.com/2011/03/vector-playing-cards.html
- Original archive: https://storage.googleapis.com/google-code-archive-downloads/v2/code.google.com/vector-playing-cards/SVG-cards-1.3.zip
